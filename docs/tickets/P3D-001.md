# P3D-001 — Particle systems

- **Status:** PARTIAL (2026-10-19)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Module:** P3D / Render
- **Files:** `src/Render/ParticleSystem.*`, `src/Level.cpp` (factories, placement, drawing),
  `src/Game.cpp` (`loadLevel`: the level's effects file), `src/Render/CompositeModel.*`
  (`GetEffectTransform`)

## What is done

Steam over vents, the smoke column, the tire fire's smoke and flames, torches, a spring's
steam: the particle effects the level's animated objects carry (19 systems on level 1).

- **Read by hand** (the generated parser has no particle chunks): a factory
  (`ParticleSystemFactory`, 0x15800: version, name, frame rate, ...) holds sprite emitters
  (0x15806: version, name, the sprites' shader, then how the sprites turn -- a four-letter
  mode, `SPEC`, `MOAL`, `RAND`, and an angle -- then their texture's frames: a mode,
  `NONE`, `RAND` or `LIN`, how many, and how many a second), each with three animations
  whose channels are, by their four-letter names:
  - `ParticleAnimation` (0x15808), over a particle's life (its frames span the life):
    `ALOL` alpha (0-255), `SZOL` size, `SPOL` speed, `WEOL` weight, `SNOL` spin, as
    multipliers;
  - `EmitterAnimation` (0x15809): `NUM` particles, `LIF` life in frames (`LIFV` either
    way), `SPD` speed per frame (`SPDV`), `SIZ` size (`SIZV`), `GRA` gravity, `WGT` weight,
    `SPI` spin, `DRA` drag, `ALP` alpha, `CLR` colour, `TRA` the emitter's offset;
  - `GeneratorAnimation` (0x1580A): `WDT`, `HGT` the area emitted from, `HOSP`, `VESP`
    the spread of directions, `RDVA`.
  A `ParticleSystem` (0x15801: version, name, factory) names its factory.
- **Placed** by the animated object (`Anim`) whose drawable's effect list (0x4517, each
  0x4518: name, translucent, joint) names the system: it stands where that joint is. An
  effect-only skeleton (a vent's steam) is placed by its root's rest pose, which the props
  of a static model ignore (`CompositeModel::GetEffectTransform`); and a drawable without
  props now keeps its skeleton.
- **Simulated**: given off `RAT` a frame, at most `NUM` alive (below), from the emitter's area,
  straight up within the spreads, at `SPD`; moved on by the speed over life and gravity;
  drawn as camera-facing sprites with the billboard program, alpha and size over their
  lives, blended as their shader says.

- **Rate and colour over time** (2026-10-19): `RAT`, the emission rate over the
  emitter's own timeline, multiplies it (an explosion's debris come in one burst); an
  effect keyed to its owner's animation (a wasp's) reads that timeline at the owner's
  frame, the others run it on their own clock. `COOL`, the colour over a particle's life,
  multiplies its colour (the smoke column darkens as it rises). Additive sprites blend
  weighted by their alpha: a wasp's sparks, given off forever and at full strength, added
  up to a white square where it blew up.

- **Bursts the game sets off** (2026-10-19): a one-emitter factory made in code
  (`ParticleFactory::Burst`) for sprites given off all at once (`ParticleSystem::Burst`):
  sparks off a car's impact (`spark3_m`, up to 30, falling) and dust under a landing jump
  (`smoke2_m`, 10, spreading). The effects file's shaders the world lacks are loaded with
  its textures. Checked: a dust cloud under Homer as he lands; a flash on the bonnet as the
  car hits a kerb.

- **Breakables' effects**: an `AnimDynamicPhysics` drawable's effect list names its
  breaking's particle systems (a vending machine's, a crate's dust, a stop sign's sparks,
  a phone stop's): nothing until it breaks, then keyed to its breaking animation's frame,
  giving off no more once it has played (`ParticleSystem::Update`'s `emit`). Checked: a
  puff of smoke out of a vending machine Homer kicks.

- **Gravity and bursts** (2026-10-20): `GRA` is m/s², up (debris fall at -2 to -6, flames
  rise at 3); it was read per frame per frame and down, and every falling piece flew to
  the sky. The timeline's rate is averaged over each step's span, so a short burst is not
  stepped over. The props' hit effects (`InstParticleSystem`, 0x3001001) play: see
  PHYS-011.

## What it took besides

- **The level's effects file** (`art/l01_fx.p3d`) was never read; it holds the textures
  the effects draw with (`smoke3.bmp`), so the tire fire's smoke drew whatever a shader
  falls back on. Its textures are now loaded with the level (not its shaders, which could
  take the world's names).
- **Images always decoded as RGBA**: asked for "as stored", a palette PNG's transparency
  (`tRNS`) came back as three opaque channels.
- **The fog no longer mixes into the alpha**: every transparent texel had a little
  opacity, and overlapping puffs showed as squares. This also gave the sky its clouds
  back: its layers were the "scattered pale panels".
- `Matrix4x4::operator*(Vector3)` (and `Vector4`) read the column-major matrix by rows: the
  transpose, translation lost. Fixed; the only other user, `LineRenderer::DrawBox`, drew
  every debug box about the origin and now draws them in place (checked: the player's).
- Two files can define a factory of the same name; the first is kept, or running systems
  would point at a destroyed one (a crash on level 1).

- **A texture of frames**: a flame's (`fireSeq.bmp`, 128 by 32) is a strip of four
  frames side by side, as are the wasps' arcs and the barrel's and debris' bits. Each
  sprite shows one: at random, changing so many times a second (`RAND`), in turn
  (`LIN`), or the first (`NONE`). Drawn whole, the four flames of a strip stood side by
  side in every sprite.

- **How many are given off** (2026-10-20): `RAT` particles a frame (at the factory's
  frame rate), as the emitter's timeline has it where it stands, and at most `NUM` alive
  at once. The bursts give it away: a bin's apples come at 7 a frame for a `NUM` of 7,
  bananas 6 and 6, bones 8 and 8; the phone booth's bits at 30 for 30; the vending
  machine's cans 15 and 15. Read before as a multiplier of the rate that keeps `NUM`
  alive, a bin gave up an apple or two, and a wrecked car's fire (two emitters of 100
  flames, 2 and 3 a frame, a sixth of a second each) was 200 flames added up to a white
  glare; it is some 15 now, under its black smoke.
