# FX-004 — Shadow mapping (a directional depth map)

- **Status:** DONE (2026-10-21)
- **Priority:** P3
- **Module:** Post-FX
- **Depends on:** FX-000
- **Note:** formerly RENDER-012
- **Files:** `src/Render/ShadowMap.{h,cpp}`, `assets/shaders/world.frag`, `src/Level.{h,cpp}`
  (`GetSunDirection`), `src/Game.cpp` (`renderShadows`, `drawShadowCasters`)

## Context
Nothing cast a shadow from the sun. The world's own shadows are in its vertex colours
(prelit): what has none is what moves.

## Done
- `ShadowMap`: a 2048 x 2048 depth map (no colour) from the level's sun, orthographic, about
  the player (40 m by default), moved a texel at a time so that its edges do not crawl.
- The casters, drawn into it from the sun (colour writes off, both sides): the player, the
  mission's people and the level's, the mission's cars, the player's car, the police, the
  traffic.
- The receiver: the world's shader (`world.frag`), on the unlit world only (the cars' lit
  meshes are drawn in their own space), nine taps for a soft edge, darkened by up to 60 %
  times the strength.
- The sun: the level's "sun" light group's directional light (`Level::GetSunDirection`). Its
  direction is given **towards** the sun (level 1: y +0.39); the light goes the other way.
  Checked on level 1: Homer's and the passers-by's shadows fall away from the sun.
- Off by default (the game's characters and cars have blob shadows, not these): the
  Post-FX window sets its strength and reach.

## Note
If the sun's direction is towards the sun, the cars' lighting (`world.frag`:
`dot(n, -lightDirections[i])`) lights their undersides rather than their roofs. To be
checked on a car, against the game.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A depth-only frame buffer from the sun, its view-projection orthographic.
- [x] The casters drawn into it before the scene.
- [x] The world's shader reads it (its own texture unit) and darkens what is in shadow, with
      a bias and soft edges.
