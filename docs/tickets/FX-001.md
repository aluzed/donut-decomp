# FX-001 — Bloom (two-pass gaussian blur)

- **Status:** DONE (2026-10-21)
- **Priority:** P3
- **Module:** Post-FX
- **Depends on:** FX-000
- **Files:** `src/Render/PostProcess.{h,cpp}`, `assets/shaders/bloom_bright.frag`,
  `assets/shaders/blur.frag`, `assets/shaders/postprocess.frag`

## Context
`postprocess.frag` sketched a bloom in its one pass: the bright parts, blurred across and
down in the same fragment. A real bloom blurs in passes, at a lower resolution.

## Done
- The post-process work out of `Game` into `PostProcess` (`src/Render/PostProcess.*`): the
  scene's frame buffer, the full-screen quad, the shaders, the settings, the Post-FX
  window's controls. `Game` calls `BeginScene` and `EndScene`; its seven members for it are
  one.
- The bloom: the scene's parts brighter than a threshold into a half-size frame buffer
  (`bloom_bright.frag`), blurred across then down (`blur.frag`, nine taps), back and forth
  between two frame buffers as many passes as set, added to the scene in the last pass.
  The one-pass sketch is gone.
- Off by default (the game has none): the Post-FX window sets its strength, threshold and
  passes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Two half-size frame buffers, blurred back and forth.
- [x] A bright pass at the threshold (0.7 by default).
- [x] Its strength and width set in the Post-FX window; added in the last pass.
- [x] The old one-pass bloom removed.
