## Contexte

Les 5 chunks d'éclairage sont **déjà entièrement décrits dans `p3d.json`** :

- `Light` (0x13000) : `version u32`, `name string`, `color vec4`, `intensity float` ;
- `LightDirection` (0x13001) : `direction vec3` ;

Nécessaire pour phares de véhicules et lumières dynamiques.

## The levels' lights (2026-10-21)

**The data.** 346 lights (`Light`, 0x13000) in 72 files: the seven terrains and the
bonus game's tracks each have a light group "sun" (`LightGroup`, 0x2380: its name, a
count, the lights' names) of four: an ambient light and three directional ones. A
light is its name, version, type (0 ambient, 2 directional), colour, three
attenuations, whether on; a directional one's direction is a child (`LightDirection`,
0x13001), its position another (0x13002, unused here). Level 1's: ambient grey 0x59,
two of 0x8C from ahead on either side, 0x59 from behind; level 3's and 4's are darker.

**Lit.** The world is lit already, by its vertex colours: it is drawn unlit. The cars
(the player's, the missions', the traffic, the police, the bonus game's) and the
characters are lit by the level's "sun": a car's material says how (`LIT`, and its
ambient, diffuse and emissive colours: a car's body has no ambient colour, it is lit
only where the sun falls), the characters fully. The colours are read on the consoles'
scale, 128 the full light: none of the game's goes past 140, and read out of 255 a car's
body came out a dull maroon. A deduction, not a figure from the code.

**Fixed with it.**
- Meshes carry their normals (`NormalList`), turned with the prop (`normalRotation`);
  a character's normal turns with its bones (it was lit as in its bind pose).
- A car's body was see-through: drawn in the opaque pass with blending on, its
  texture's alpha (its reflection's mask) let the world through. Composite models
  draw their opaque pass unblended.
- `ShaderProgram::SetUniformValue` looked a name up with `map[]`: a name not among
  the program's active uniforms (an array's element after the first, or a uniform
  the program does not have) read as location 0 and wrote over that uniform -- GL
  errors, then a crash. It now asks GL for it once, and -1 is ignored.

Not done: the cars' reflections (their `spheremap` shaders' `REFL` environment map,
blended in by `ENVB`).
