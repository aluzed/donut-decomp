# P3D-010 — Chunks StaticProp

- **Status:** DONE (2026-10-21: read by `P3D::StateProp`, played by the level's breakables and the wasps)
- **Priority:** P2
- **Module:** P3D
- **Depends on:** —
- **Files:** `dev/codegen/p3d.json:916-919` (`StaticPropData` **déjà décrit**, minimal), `src/P3D/P3DChunk.h:191-196` (ChunkType), `src/P3D/P3D.generated.{h,cpp}`

## Contexte

Famille StaticProp (props animés/à états du décor), état inégal :

- `StaticPropData` (0x8020000) : **déjà dans `p3d.json`** mais minimal
  (`version u32`, `name string` uniquement) et non régénéré → à compléter ;
- `StaticPropStateData` (0x8020001), `StaticPropVisibilityData` (0x8020002),
  `StaticPropFrameControllerData` (0x8020003), `StaticPropEventData` (0x8020004),
  `StaticPropCallbackData` (0x8020005) : déclarés dans l'enum `ChunkType` mais **absents
  de `p3d.json`** → à reverser.

## Approche

1. Reverser la hiérarchie : `StaticPropData` agrège vraisemblablement des
   `StaticPropStateData` (un par état), chaque état contenant `StaticPropVisibilityData`,
   `StaticPropFrameControllerData`, `StaticPropEventData`, `StaticPropCallbackData`.
2. Compléter `StaticPropData` et décrire les 5 sous-chunks dans `p3d.json` avec la
   grammaire en place (`child`/`children`).
3. Lancer `dev/codegen/run.bat`, vérifier les classes et `Load()`.
4. Brancher la lecture dans le chargement de niveau (`Level.cpp`) au moins en parsing
   (le déclenchement des events/callbacks au gameplay peut être un ticket séparé).

## Critères d'acceptation

- [ ] `StaticPropData` complété + les 5 sous-chunks décrits dans `p3d.json` et générés.
- [ ] La hiérarchie état→(visibility/frameController/event/callback) est correctement parsée.
- [ ] Un `.p3d` contenant des StaticProp se parse sans assert.
- [ ] Les états et leur visibilité sont accessibles via getters générés.

## Done (2026-10-21)

**Where they are.** 290 state props in the data: 263 in an `AnimDynamicPhysics`'
object wrapper (0x3F0000E > 0x3F00010: every breakable of the levels -- crates,
vending machines, gas pumps, phone stops, lamps, fences, hay bales, signs), 27 on
their own in the effects and mission files (the wasp camera and its shield, the
saucer's beam, the bomb barrel).

**Their layout**, read by `P3D::StateProp` (`src/P3D/StateProp.*`, by hand: the code
generator is TOOL-004's):

| Chunk | Fields |
|---|---|
| `StaticPropData` 0x8020000 | version, name, object factory name, state count |
| `StaticPropStateData` 0x8020001 | name, auto transition, its next state, the counts of its drawables, controllers, events and callbacks, after how long (seconds) |
| `StaticPropVisibilityData` 0x8020002 | drawable, visible |
| `StaticPropFrameControllerData` 0x8020003 | controller (`PTRN_` pose, `EFX_` particles, `BQG_` billboards, `TEX_` texture frames, `PVIS` visibility), cyclic, cycles, hold, first and last frame, speed |
| `StaticPropEventData` 0x8020004 | event name, the state it leads to, its number (HIT 8, STOMP 10, VEHICLE_HIT 11, DESTROYED 7, FEATHER_TOUCH 9) |
| `StaticPropCallbackData` 0x8020005 | name, its number, when (seconds into the state) |

Times are seconds in the state, frames 30 a second: the wasp's coming in, 75 frames at
twice their speed, goes on after 1.25 s, as its state says. Callbacks go by number,
their names vary (11 is called SPAWN_5_COINS_Z and SPAWN_25_COINS_Z): 0 remove from
the world, 1 five coins, 2 remove the collision volume, 3 fire (the wasp's), 4 kill
speed, 5 ten coins, 7 twenty, 8 radiate force, 10 object destroyed, 11 five coins, 12
one coin, 13 cola destroyed, 14 camera shake, 16 remove the second volume.

**Played.** A level's breakable with states goes from state to state on what is done
to it: struck (HIT), kicked from a jump (STOMP), driven into (VEHICLE_HIT). A crate is
struck three times before it gives, ten coins spilled each time (thirty in all), twenty
driven into; a vending machine, a phone stop likewise, as their states say. Each state
shows its drawables, poses the object along its `PTRN_` frames, runs its particle
effects along their `EFX_` frames, goes on by itself when it says so, and calls back:
coins spilled about it to be picked up (`CollectibleManager::DropCoins`), its body
removed (the car that hit it given its speed back), itself gone. One without states
still plays its animation through once. The wasps read theirs through it too.

Not played: the callbacks that are not the level's objects' (fire, kill speed, radiate
force, camera shake, the cola truck's); `BQG_`, `TEX_` and `PVIS` controllers beyond
the visibility the state sets.
