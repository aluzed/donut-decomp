# P3D-004 — Animations of props, gags and level objects

- **Status:** MOSTLY DONE (2026-10-19)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Module:** P3D / Render
- **Files:** `src/Render/SkinAnimation.*` (`FromP3D`), `src/Render/CompositeModel.*` (`Pose`),
  `src/Render/SceneSkins.*`, `src/Level.cpp` (`CompositeModel_Anim`), `src/Game.cpp`
  (`drawGags`), `src/P3D/P3D.generated.*` (`Vector1Channel`)

## What is done

- **One conversion** from a P3D animation to per-joint tracks, `SkinAnimation::FromP3D`,
  for the characters, gags and level objects. It reads translations from Vector3, Vector2
  and Vector1 channels (the last two move along two or one axis, the rest held at
  `constants`; `Vector1Channel` was added to the parser) and rotations from quaternion and
  compressed quaternion channels. Tracks can be played once, holding their last frame.
- **`CompositeModel::Pose(frame, loop)`**: a composite drawable keeps its skeleton and its
  pose animation (`PTRN_*`, not the `TEX_*` texture animations the same files carry), and
  moves its props to any frame.
- **Gags** play their animation when triggered (GAME-010), and the characters in their
  scenes (`SceneSkins`) with it.
- **Level objects**: `Anim` and `AnimCollision` chunks were not loaded at all. Each is now a
  composite drawable put together from what it names in its file (skeleton, meshes, and
  the animation its frame controller plays) and drawn with the level. They go round at
  their own rate, but for doorbells and switches (`DB_*`, `*switch*`), which stand at
  their first frame.

- **Doorbells**: ringing one plays, once, its button (`DB_quimby`) and the switch its
  action locator names second (`quimby_switch6`), which then go back to where they stood.
  Checked from the log (one of each started at Quimby's door); not watched on screen, the
  button's animation is two frames long.

- **Breakables** (`AnimDynamicPhysics`: 42 on level 1, crates, gas pumps, vending
  machines, phone stops, fences): their model's animation is their breaking. They stand at
  its first frame; the player's car running into one (within 2.5m, over 15 km/h) plays it
  once and leaves it broken, with a thud and a shake. Checked: the gas station's two pumps
  blown off their bases; on level 6 the autopilot's car smashed one in passing. They still
  have no body (PHYS-011): the car goes through them.

- **The player** used two of their 67 animations, walk and rest, walking at 5 m/s. Now:
  the run while moving; the jump's take-off, the time in the air and the landing; after
  8s standing still, one of the idle ones (Homer scratches, checks his shoes, dances...);
  pressing a doorbell (`hit_switch`); the dash (`loco_dash`, 8 m/s rather than 5) with the
  boost held on foot; a kick (`jump_kick`) with the action button in the air, once a jump,
  which hits a wasp in reach and breaks a breakable it lands on; on the ground beside a
  breakable or a wasp, the action button strikes (`break`) rather than jumps (checked:
  Homer kicks a gas pump off its base); a small victory when a mission is won on foot. By
  their names without the character's prefix (`loco_run` is `hom_loco_run`), so any
  playable character. A character's animation can be played once (`SetAnimation(name,
  false)`), holding its last frame.
- **Gestures in conversation**: while a line is being spoken, the player on foot and
  anyone within 8m gesture, one `*_dialogue_*` animation after another (hands in the air,
  thinking, shaking a fist), and go back to standing at rest. Non-player characters stand
  at `*_loco_idle_rest`: "the first idle" of an unordered set had Apu doing yoga behind
  his counter. Checked from the log: Apu, Marge and Homer gesture through level 1's talks.
- **Standing about**, a character with animations of its own (Apu, Lisa, Marge...) plays
  one of its idles now and then, every 12 to 25s; one borrowing Apu's (Ralph, Moe) does
  not do Apu's yoga. **Pedestrians run down** flail on their way through the air
  (`*_flail`), and walk again when their body is next used. Pedestrians stop now and then
  where they have got to (one time in three, 2 to 6 seconds) and stand at rest: about a
  third of them stand at any time. Those within 7m ahead of a car coming at more than
  20 km/h run clear of it, sideways from its path, in their dash, for a second or so
  (checked from the log: one fled from 6.9m). Checked from the log: Lisa
  swats, Apu backspins; 15 pedestrians knocked over, flailing.
- **Getting in and out of a car**: by the driver's door, the door opens, the player
  climbs in, the door shuts -- the character's three `get_into_car_*_driver` animations --
  and only then is at the wheel; out, the same the other way. No control meanwhile; a car
  that disappears (a mission restarted) leaves the player where he is. The car's own
  driver's door swings open and shut with it: cars carry no animation for it, but a hinge
  joint (`DoorDRot`), which `CompositeModel::SetJointRotation` turns. Its position says
  which side the driver is on: -X, the left in the game's left-handed space (taking +X
  for the left put Homer at the passenger's side, and the door opened away from him).
  Checked on screen, from the side and from above. `--autopilot` still seats the player at
  once.
- **At the wheel**: the player is drawn in the driver's seat, idling as drivers do
  (`in_car_idle`), where the car model's `dl` joint is. The driving animations are built
  about a point beside the car (their `Motion_Root` stands at (1.0, 0.46, -0.72) from the
  character's origin), so that root, not the origin, goes to the seat; and the character
  faces the car's way (no half turn, unlike walking). Checked from the side and from
  above: Homer behind the family convertible's windscreen, on the driver's side, hands
  on the wheel. Before, he vanished as he got in.
- **The mission cars' drivers**: each mission car is driven by the stage's driver
  (`AddStageVehicle`'s last argument: "snake", "wiggum", "eddie"...) or else the car's own
  (`SetDriver` in `scripts/cars/<car>.con`; "none" for an empty seat). They are made as
  their car first comes within 120m of the camera (as a mission NPC is, borrowing Apu's
  animations when they have none; one without a model is not tried again), seated the same
  way, and kept while their car is. Checked: Snake at the wheel of his Li'l Bandit,
  racing.
- **Brake and reversing lights**: a car file's billboards `brake1Shape`..`brake4Shape` and
  `rev1Shape`..`rev4Shape`, hung from their joints, are drawn while the car brakes (the
  brake, or the throttle against the way it rolls) or backs up. The blending is put back
  after (the HUD's text). Checked: a flare at the family convertible's tail light as it
  brakes, both reversing lights as it backs up. The traffic's light up while a car or the
  player holds them up (checked: an SUV stopped behind the player at the power plant).
- **Wheels**: the model's wheel joints (`w0`..`w3`, matched to the physics wheels by where
  they stand) roll and steer as the physics wheels do. Checked: they stay in place on a
  moving car; their turning was not made out at speed. The traffic's roll too: each car's
  wheels are turned on the shared model just before it is drawn.
- **Blinking**: a character's model carries a texture animation (`TEX_eyeball_m`, one
  entity channel naming a texture per key: `eyeball.bmp.0` to `.3` and back over nine
  frames, the textures in `art/chars/global.p3d`). Every 2.5 to 5.5s the character's eyes'
  shader draws those instead of its own, for that character's draw only (`SkinModel::Draw`
  retextures after `GetShader`, which resets a shader's texture each time). Checked on
  screen, a blink forced every second: Homer's lids come down and up.
- **The level's texture animations** (9 on level 1: the rivers' water, the pool, the goop,
  the Kwik-E-Mart's arcade screen) go round, their shader drawing one texture after
  another. One reader for these and the blinks (`TextureAnimation`). Checked: the pool's
  rippling light changes from one frame to the next.
- **A gag scene's eyes** follow its own `TEX_eyeball_m`, keyed to the scene (Grandpa's
  blinks once, as the scene opens). Not seen on screen: he is still out of view then.
- **Moving collision**: an `AnimCollision` object's `CollisionObject` (0x7010000) becomes
  kinematic bodies (77 on level 1) that move with it. Each volume names an owner, a prop
  of the object, by `GetOwnerIndex`; some volumes are in that prop's own space (the beef
  boards' boxes, about the origin), others in the world's (the power plant's platforms',
  owned by a placeholder, `__PrenotatoPerDuplicare_`, where the platform stands at its
  first frame). Either way each keeps the offset from its joint it has at that first
  frame, and is put there again every frame. Checked: the player put on a platform rides
  it up and down (5.8m, 8.4m, 8.7m, 7.5m, 5.0m, half a second apart), standing on its
  grating on screen.
- **Billboards of animated objects, and their flashing**: `BillboardQuadGroup`s inside
  `Anim` objects (82 on level 1: the fire trucks' sirens, sparkles, arrows) are quads
  about the origin, hung from a joint through the drawable's prop list (the prop's name is
  the group's); each is drawn where its joint is. A `BQG_<quad>` animation's group, named
  after its quad, has a colour channel (`CLR`, 0x121109: key frames u16, colours u32) that
  recolours the quad, round and round; WDT and HGT size it and VIS (a bool channel: start
  state, toggle frames) shows or hides it (`BillboardAnimation`, shared with the wasps;
  DIST, OFF, TRAN and ROT are not read). They showed nothing at first, nor do the level's own billboards: the billboard
  program shares the world's fragment shader but its uniforms were never set, and
  `lightmapIntensity` left at 1 multiplied each glow by a corner of its own texture,
  black, which an additive glow does not show. Checked: red siren glows on the fire
  trucks, bright and dim by turns.
- **The jump itself** did not work: the character rose 5cm and was set down the next
  frame. Nothing moved it up (`CharacterController::rise` now does, swept against
  ceilings), and every step ended with `Character::SetPosition`, which warps the
  controller onto the ground and zeroes its vertical speed. The controller now only tells
  the character where it is (`FollowController`). Checked: a 1.15m jump, with its three
  animations; the seven levels' stories under `--autopilot`, no fall through the world.

## Two things that were wrong

- **Uncompressed quaternions** are stored w first, (w, x, y, z), and the parser reads them
  into the struct in that order. The windmill's root, identity on file (-1, 0, 0, 0), came
  out as half a turn about X: the object was drawn upside down on the far side of the map.
  Compressed quaternions were already read right (the characters use them).
- A model's **transform** was left uninitialised unless something set it; the level's
  animated objects are placed by their skeleton and never set one.

- Grandpa walks into the Simpsons' living room (a gag's skins).
- A butterfly flies across the Simpsons' garden (a level `Anim`).

## Layouts found

- `Vector1Channel` (0x121102): version u32, param string[4], mapping u16 (the axis that
  moves), constants vec3, numFrames u32, frames u16[numFrames], values f32[numFrames];
  child `ChannelInterpolationMode`. Now in `p3d.json` and the parser.
- `Vector2Channel`: as above with vec2 values; mapping is the axis that holds.
- `FrameController` (0x121200), read by hand in `Level.cpp`: version u32, name (LP),
  type string[4] (`PTRN`, `EFX\0`), frame offset f32, hierarchy name (LP), animation
  name (LP). `p3d.json`'s description of it (version, name, controllerId, numTracks) is
  wrong and was not used.

## Notes

- **Vertex animation** (morph targets): one file of the game has one, `gag_blow.p3d`, the
  blow-up doll -- a gag cut from levels 3 and 6 (commented out in their scripts). Read
  (`VertexAnimation`: per primitive group, an entity channel naming a key frame per frame;
  each key a list of offsets, one per vertex) and played on a gag's mesh
  (`Mesh::OffsetGroup`, rewriting that group's vertices). Checked by placing the gag by
  hand: the doll, flat and slumped as built, stands inflated.
- Climbing into a truck (a seat over 0.5m above the hubs) plays the `_high` animations:
  checked with the family car's model swapped for the armoured van's, Homer climbs in by
  its open door and back out (no mission puts the player in one).
- The `MultiController` timing was checked and is not needed: over the seven levels, all
  341 animated objects' controllers have their animation's own length and rate. The generated
  parser read `MultiControllerTracks` as four arrays; each track is in fact its name,
  start, end and scale in turn (fixed by hand in `P3D.generated.cpp`; the codegen cannot
  describe it). Particle effects: P3D-001.
- `warrow`/`darrow` stand at the world's origin, below the map, out of sight.
- The sky (`WorldSphere`) uses the same conversion as the rest. Its "scattered pale
  panels" were the fog mixed into the alpha (P3D-001); its billboards needed the
  billboard program (RENDER-011).

## The objects the action triggers work (2026-10-21)

Some of the level's animated objects are worked by its action triggers rather than going
round by themselves (four kinds were left unhandled, logged on levels 1, 4 and 7):

- `ToggleOnOff` (the power plant's switched platforms `Splatform1/4/5Trans`, and its
  door): the action key starts or stops the object, and throws its lever, the object
  whose name ends with the trigger's target (`switch1`: `z6switch1`);
- `AutoPlayAnim` (the automatic platform `Aplatform6Trans`, the branch, level 7's twelve
  `TD..` trapdoors): moving while the player, or their car, is in the trigger;
- `AutomaticDoor` (`hdoortrans1`): opening while the player is in it, closing after;
- `PlayAnim` (the Duff truck's switch): played once.

The trigger names its object by its locator, its own way: the power plant's less "PP_"
(`PP_Splatform1`: `Splatform1Trans`; one, `PP_Splatfrom5`, misspelt), level 7's as is
(`TD02`). Names repeat about the level (Wiggum's doorbell has a `wiggum_switch4`; the
Duff truck's target `switch1` is also Skinner's `skinner_switch11`), so the object and
the lever are taken only within 60 m of the trigger (the nearest of a name), and an
object goes to one trigger only (`hdoortrans1` is the automatic door's, not switch 3's).
Unbound for want of a match: the Duff truck's object, level 7's `Tube01` (`TubeA`).

Each worked object runs on its own clock (`Level::DriveAnimatedObject`: speed 1, 0 or
-1; round and round, or held at either end for a door); the moving collision follows
as before. Checked: the bindings logged (each platform its `z6switchN`); switch 1 thrown,
`Splatform1Trans` on, its moving joint from 4 m up to the ground at its middle frame;
level 1's power plant mission (the couplings) still won under `--autopilot`.
