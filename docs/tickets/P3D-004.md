# P3D-004 — Animations of props, gags and level objects

- **Status:** PARTIAL (2026-10-19)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Module:** P3D / Render
- **Files:** `src/Render/SkinAnimation.*` (`FromP3D`), `src/Render/CompositeModel.*` (`Pose`),
  `src/Render/SceneSkins.*`, `src/Level.cpp` (`CompositeModel_Anim`), `src/Game.cpp`
  (`drawGags`), `src/P3D/P3D.generated.*` (`Vector1Channel`)

## What is done

- **One conversion** from a P3D animation to per-joint tracks, `SkinAnimation::FromP3D`,
  for the characters, gags and level objects. It reads translations from Vector3, Vector2
  and Vector1 channels (the last two move along two or one axis, the rest held at
  `constants`; `Vector1Channel` was added to the parser) and rotations from quaternion and
  compressed quaternion channels. Tracks can be played once, holding their last frame.
- **`CompositeModel::Pose(frame, loop)`**: a composite drawable keeps its skeleton and its
  pose animation (`PTRN_*`, not the `TEX_*` texture animations the same files carry), and
  moves its props to any frame.
- **Gags** play their animation when triggered (GAME-010), and the characters in their
  scenes (`SceneSkins`) with it.
- **Level objects**: `Anim` and `AnimCollision` chunks were not loaded at all. Each is now a
  composite drawable put together from what it names in its file (skeleton, meshes, and
  the animation its frame controller plays) and drawn with the level. They go round at
  their own rate, but for doorbells and switches (`DB_*`, `*switch*`), which stand at
  their first frame.

- **Doorbells**: ringing one plays, once, its button (`DB_quimby`) and the switch its
  action locator names second (`quimby_switch6`), which then go back to where they stood.
  Checked from the log (one of each started at Quimby's door); not watched on screen, the
  button's animation is two frames long.

- **Breakables** (`AnimDynamicPhysics`: 42 on level 1, crates, gas pumps, vending
  machines, phone stops, fences): their model's animation is their breaking. They stand at
  its first frame; the player's car running into one (within 2.5m, over 15 km/h) plays it
  once and leaves it broken, with a thud and a shake. Checked: the gas station's two pumps
  blown off their bases; on level 6 the autopilot's car smashed one in passing. They still
  have no body (PHYS-011): the car goes through them.

- **The player** used two of their 67 animations, walk and rest, walking at 5 m/s. Now:
  the run while moving; the jump's take-off, the time in the air and the landing; after
  8s standing still, one of the idle ones (Homer scratches, checks his shoes, dances...);
  pressing a doorbell (`hit_switch`); a small victory when a mission is won on foot. By
  their names without the character's prefix (`loco_run` is `hom_loco_run`), so any
  playable character. A character's animation can be played once (`SetAnimation(name,
  false)`), holding its last frame.
- **Gestures in conversation**: while a line is being spoken, the player on foot and
  anyone within 8m gesture, one `*_dialogue_*` animation after another (hands in the air,
  thinking, shaking a fist), and go back to standing at rest. Non-player characters stand
  at `*_loco_idle_rest`: "the first idle" of an unordered set had Apu doing yoga behind
  his counter. Checked from the log: Apu, Marge and Homer gesture through level 1's talks.
- **Standing about**, a character with animations of its own (Apu, Lisa, Marge...) plays
  one of its idles now and then, every 12 to 25s; one borrowing Apu's (Ralph, Moe) does
  not do Apu's yoga. **Pedestrians run down** flail on their way through the air
  (`*_flail`), and walk again when their body is next used. Checked from the log: Lisa
  swats, Apu backspins; 15 pedestrians knocked over, flailing.
- **The jump itself** did not work: the character rose 5cm and was set down the next
  frame. Nothing moved it up (`CharacterController::rise` now does, swept against
  ceilings), and every step ended with `Character::SetPosition`, which warps the
  controller onto the ground and zeroes its vertical speed. The controller now only tells
  the character where it is (`FollowController`). Checked: a 1.15m jump, with its three
  animations; the seven levels' stories under `--autopilot`, no fall through the world.

## Two things that were wrong

- **Uncompressed quaternions** are stored w first, (w, x, y, z), and the parser reads them
  into the struct in that order. The windmill's root, identity on file (-1, 0, 0, 0), came
  out as half a turn about X: the object was drawn upside down on the far side of the map.
  Compressed quaternions were already read right (the characters use them).
- A model's **transform** was left uninitialised unless something set it; the level's
  animated objects are placed by their skeleton and never set one.

## Checked

- Level 1's swing set topples over (a gag, Vector1 and Vector2 channels).
- Grandpa walks into the Simpsons' living room (a gag's skins).
- A butterfly flies across the Simpsons' garden (a level `Anim`).
- The doorbells stand where their triggers are, and each `Anim`/`AnimCollision` of level 1
  at its first frame matches its rest pose wherever that is set.
- The seven levels load and play under `--autopilot`, no crash.

## Layouts found

- `Vector1Channel` (0x121102): version u32, param string[4], mapping u16 (the axis that
  moves), constants vec3, numFrames u32, frames u16[numFrames], values f32[numFrames];
  child `ChannelInterpolationMode`. Now in `p3d.json` and the parser.
- `Vector2Channel`: as above with vec2 values; mapping is the axis that holds.
- `FrameController` (0x121200), read by hand in `Level.cpp`: version u32, name (LP),
  type string[4] (`PTRN`, `EFX\0`), frame offset f32, hierarchy name (LP), animation
  name (LP). `p3d.json`'s description of it (version, name, controllerId, numTracks) is
  wrong and was not used.
